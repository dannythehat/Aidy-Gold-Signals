01 · The process

Six gates. No permanent pass.

A source is not approved because it has a large following, a polished feed or an impressive screenshot. We want the complete record: what was called, what changed after entry, how the trade closed and whether that performance survives over time.

01

Discover the source

We find Gold signal sources across free groups, paid VIP channels and established trading communities.

What mattersThe source has to be observable in real time. We judge what members actually receive, not a curated results page.
02

Store the complete record

Entries, stops, targets, edits, cancellations, partial closes and final outcomes stay attached to the same trade idea.

What mattersA losing trade stays in the database. A deleted message or changed instruction does not erase the earlier record.
03

Audit performance over time

Sources are judged across a meaningful history, not one strong day or one unusually good month.

What mattersConsistency, risk behaviour and the shape of wins and losses matter more than a headline win rate.
04

Learn how that source communicates

Every signal source has its own shorthand. Smart Signals has to understand what “move SL”, “delete pending”, “secure partial” or an edited post means for an existing trade.

What mattersContext. A later message must remain connected to the correct open trade rather than being treated as a new idea.
05

Approve only what survives the gates

Only sources with sufficiently strong evidence reach Smart Signals Trading.

What mattersApproval is selective. A source can be tracked for weeks and still never become available in the app.
06

Audit again next month

Live sources remain under review. If performance deteriorates or behaviour becomes unreliable, that source can lose its place.

What mattersYesterday's reputation does not override today's evidence. Sources have to keep earning access to the app.

02 · The audit

What goes into the database.

The audit is deliberately broader than “did TP hit?”. We keep the surrounding instructions because trade management is part of signal quality.

Stored

The complete signal

  • Entry or entry range
  • Initial stop loss
  • Every take-profit target
  • Edits and follow-up messages
  • Partial closes and final close
  • Final profit or loss outcome
Rewarded

What earns approval

  • Sustained profitable behaviour
  • Clear and actionable instructions
  • Reasonable stop discipline
  • Reliable trade management
  • Results that survive repeated review
  • Instructions Smart Signals can follow safely
Rejected

What keeps a source out

  • Cherry-picked or unverifiable history
  • Frequent unexplained deletions
  • Weak long-term risk-adjusted results
  • Instructions that cannot be followed reliably
  • Performance that deteriorates after approval
  • Inconsistent or unsafe trade behaviour

03 · After approval

A trade is a lifecycle, not one message.

Once a source is approved, Smart Signals Trading has to keep following the instruction after the entry is placed. That is where many signal-copying systems become unreliable.

01 · Signal

Receive

02 · Structure

Understand

03 · Update

Follow

Edits, cancellations and management messages update that record.

04 · Protect

Manage

Stops and management instructions remain part of the live trade state.

05 · Outcome

Record

The final result returns to the database and affects future approval.

Prove it.
Then keep proving it.

That is the central rule behind Smart Signals. We are not trying to predict which source looks impressive. We are building a living evidence base that can remove weak sources, retain strong ones and keep the app focused on the signals that continue to justify their place.
