# Day 53 Step 2 — Successor information state

Any successor to the Step 2 equivalence qualification must disclose the following pre-existing information:

1. The frozen Step 2 sampling frame and nested selectors had a structural session-coverage shortfall: London maximum 48 versus required 50; New York maximum 44 versus required 50.
2. This shortfall was established without inspecting HistData/Twelve paired market values for the target experiment.
3. No achievable-sample 20/50/40 equivalence scores were computed or inspected.
4. No Twelve Data vendor calls were used to produce the formal Step 2 result.

Therefore a successor sampling-frame design is **coverage-informed but result-uninformed** with respect to the target market-value equivalence scores. It must not describe itself as fully blind to the prior sampling-frame outcome.

If the hypothesis and singleton 20/50/40 target remain unchanged while only the sampling frame changes, the preferred ledger representation is a child trial in the existing research family with an explicit parent/reference to the completed Step 2 attempt. A new research family is required only if the research question or hypothesis materially changes.
