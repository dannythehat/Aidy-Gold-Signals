# Day 27 — Point-in-time market regimes, minimum sample gates and honest unknown news

## Implemented data path

- `src/aidy/strategy_lab_regimes.py`: source-constrained, deterministic **trend/range/transitional**, **high/normal/low volatility** and **DST-aware London/New York overlap, individual and outside hours** labels. Each label on five-minute gold bars is based ONLY on fully closed observations available by its exact UTC decision timestamp. Its past window is a predefined **12 closed five-minute returns**; no future prices, whole-sample fit or regime relabelling once outcomes are known.
- Trend computed using trailing Kaufman-style directional efficiency = absolute close-price displacement divided by sum of absolute close-to-close changes over 12 completed M5 changes. Fixed *research policy* thresholds, NOT optimised to any original strategy: `>=0.60` for up/down trend (sign identified from current vs window opening close), `<=0.35` for range, otherwise transitional. Flat with zero historical movement is range. Volatility: trailing mean absolute close-to-close return expressed in basis points, `<3` low, `>=10` high, otherwise normal. These thresholds are illustrative not empirically broker-calibrated; the source and policy SHA prove which definition was used.
- London/New York trading-session descriptors use real `Europe/London` and `America/New_York` IANA timezone and DST rules with declared 08:00–17:00 local nominal windows (overlap, London-only, New-York-only, other). These are analytical definitions, not an assertion that gold OTC liquidity follows exchange-opening boundaries or a provider's broker server clock.
- Economic news status is **`unknown_unqualified_calendar`**, because no independently sourced, as-of-available event calendar has been qualified in this build. It can never be inferred as “no news”; news performance metrics remain `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE_NO_NEWS_CONCLUSIONS` even when sample counts are large. This is an intentional incomplete dimension, not a false news outcome.
- `Day26 SealedOOS(history).review_development()` supplies the ONLY candidate trial: **4 expanding-fold sets of 12 genuinely walk-forward validation decisions**, one train-only model direction per fold, 48 validation decisions total. Day27 labels each as of its actual decision timestamp, joins every decision exactly once to its already-known forward two-bar BID-close-change diagnostic and naive always-up hit rates. No further model fitting, no live trade claims, and **0 final sealed holdout decisions consumed**.
- Per bucket, **minimum 12 validation decisions** is required to show retrospective model and always-up directional hit rate. With 0–11 samples (or source-unqualified news) return `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`/`SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE_NO_NEWS_CONCLUSIONS` and null performance metrics, not fake zeros, claims of significant differences or retrospective profits. All buckets, including empty buckets, are listed. Each of the four stratification dimensions independently partitions all 48 source-matched decisions without dropping observations.
- `scripts/strategy_lab/day27_real_market_regime_audit.py` reuses the genuine source-qualified Day07 BigQuery historical M1-to-M5 reader on **2025-01-06 11:00–21:00 UTC**, validates real 600 native source M1 -> 120 fully closed M5, runs two independent read-only queries and compares both source digest and exact result JSON; machine-readable audit written as GitHub Actions artefact. `tests/test_strategy_lab_regimes.py` and `tests/test_strategy_lab_day27_real_history.py` enforce future-price causality metamorphic tests, DST transition, source data tampering/gaps, minimum-sample logic, no news inference, deterministic SHA and full pipeline. Earlier Day26 OOS, Day25 costs and Day22 historical source regression also gate.

## Scientific scope and commercial meaning

This is a *real-history point-in-time regime-descriptor and diagnostic-breakdown engine*. It does **NOT** measure costed real strategy trade P&L or demonstrate genuine strategy profitability: all directional predictions are from Day26's intentionally simple historical BID-close sign toy rule. Day26 reported it only hit 8/20 final holdout directions, equal to always-up. Day27 only examines registered *development validation folds*, not that already revealed holdout or a new profitable candidate.

Economic event calendars are unknown; cross-venue executable gold spread/ask, slippage, commissions, borrowing, broker account parity and independently time-stamped real-time news feeds are not available to this build. It cannot declare profitable behaviour in “news”, precise broker sessions, or unseen neighbouring chart intervals without future qualified tests. The regime thresholds are governance parameters not discovery-optimised parameters. Any change of definitions is a **different trial** and must be recorded before using untouched future data.

Owner's requested later **multi-timeframe robustness / opt-in optimisation** remains recorded and must use separately predeclared variants, fair execution and withheld test data. Customer strategy licensing, referral or revenue-sharing terms are an idea to revisit after core build and require consent/contractual commercial permissions; **no deal or customer right is inferred or activated**.
